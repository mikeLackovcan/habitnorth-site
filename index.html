Questions

What happens when I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. One miss is forgiven; the next day is a recovery day and pays double. Only two misses in a row end a run.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your phone without one. An account is only for a cloud backup or moving to a new phone.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no tracking, no subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Erase everything removes it from the phone. Profile → Cloud → Delete account removes the account and backup. Details.
