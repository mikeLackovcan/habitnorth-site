Privacy Policy — HabitNorth

Effective 2026-09-27. Applies to the HabitNorth Android app published by Michal Fecko ("we", "the app").

The short version

Everything you track stays on your phone. If you choose to create an account, we store your email address and, when you press "Back up now" or the app backs up on its own while signed in, a copy of your data, so you can restore it on another phone. We do not sell data, show ads, or share anything with anyone else.

What the app stores on your phone

Habits, tasks, completions, notes, settings and optional photos you attach to a habit. This lives in a local database on the device and is never sent anywhere unless you use the account features below. You can export it as a file, or erase all of it, from the Profile screen at any time.

What we store in the cloud, and only if you sign in

  • Your email address and a password hash, or your Google account identity

if you choose "Continue with Google". Used only to sign you in.

  • A backup of your app data, only when you press "Back up now". It is the

same content as the file export. It is stored under your account and only your account can read it.

These are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in Google's data centres. Firebase's own privacy terms apply to that storage: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a report containing the error, the app version and the device model may be sent to Sentry so we can fix it. It contains no habit data, no email and no identifier that names you.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics of your behaviour, no selling or sharing of data, no tracking across apps or websites, no location.

Deleting your data

  • On the phone: Profile → "Erase everything". Removes every habit, task

and entry from the device. Your cloud backup, if you made one, stays.

  • In the cloud: Profile → Cloud → "Delete account". Removes the account

and the backup immediately. Nothing on the phone is touched.

  • To be gone completely, do both.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email support@habitnorth.com from the address you registered with, subject "Delete my HabitNorth account". The account and its backup are deleted within 7 days and you receive a confirmation. Nothing is retained afterwards.

Photos you attach to habits

A photo attached to a habit stays on the phone as a file. It is not part of the backup and does not move to another device.

Permissions the app asks for

Notifications and exact alarms, so reminders arrive at the time you set. Photos, only if you attach a picture to a habit. Nothing else.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version ships with the next app update. Questions: support@habitnorth.com
